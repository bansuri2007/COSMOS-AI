# 🌌 COSMOS AI

### One Universe. Infinite Ways to Learn.

**COSMOS AI** is an AI-powered, gamified, and inclusive learning
platform that aims to make education more personalized, engaging, and
accessible. It supports diverse learning needs through adaptive study
plans, interactive learning missions, alternative explanations, and
accessibility-focused design---including support for learners with
disabilities.

> **Our vision:** Help every learner explore knowledge, overcome
> learning barriers, and progress in a way that works for them.

## ✨ Key Features

-   🧠 **AI Learning Coach** --- Offers explanations, hints, and guided
    support for challenging concepts.
-   🎯 **Personalized Study Plans** --- Recommends learning activities
    based on goals, progress, and areas that need more practice.
-   🌌 **Immersive Learning Missions** --- Turns subjects and concepts
    into interactive challenges and discoveries.
-   🎮 **Gamified Learning** --- Uses missions, achievements, progress
    maps, and meaningful rewards to encourage learning.
-   🔄 **Adaptive Practice** --- Offers alternative explanations and
    targeted practice when learners need extra support.
-   ♿ **Inclusive Learning Design** --- Aims to support screen readers,
    keyboard navigation, captions, adjustable text, contrast options,
    reduced motion, and alternative interaction methods.
-   🛠️ **Learning Through Creation** --- Encourages learners to apply
    knowledge through projects, simulations, and practical challenges.
-   📊 **Progress Insights** --- Helps learners understand demonstrated
    strengths and concepts that may need further practice.

## 🎯 Who Is It For?

COSMOS AI is designed for diverse learners, including:

-   Students seeking engaging and personalized learning.
-   Learners who need additional practice or alternative explanations.
-   Students with disabilities and different accessibility requirements.
-   Teachers looking to support varied learning needs.
-   Parents who want clear insights into learning progress.

## ♿ Accessibility by Design

Accessibility is a core design goal, not an add-on. COSMOS AI aims to
provide multiple ways to access content and complete essential
activities, including:

-   Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls.
-   Screen-reader compatibility and semantic page structure.
-   Captions and transcripts for audio and video content.
-   Adjustable text size and readable layouts.
-   High-contrast options and information that does not rely on colour
    alone.
-   Reduced-motion and sensory-friendly settings.
-   Alternative interaction methods where appropriate.

Accessibility should be tested with assistive technologies and, wherever
possible, with feedback from people who use them. Learners have
different needs, so settings should be customizable rather than assigned
based on assumptions.

## 🧭 How It Works

1.  **Set a learning goal** --- Choose a subject, topic, or skill.
2.  **Explore a mission** --- Enter an interactive learning activity.
3.  **Learn** --- Explore explanations, examples, and guided activities.
4.  **Practise** --- Answer questions and receive feedback.
5.  **Adapt** --- Revisit difficult concepts through different
    explanations or targeted practice.
6.  **Track progress** --- Review learning progress and choose the next
    useful activity.

## 🌱 Our Principles

-   **Personalization:** Adapt learning support to learners' goals and
    demonstrated needs.
-   **Inclusion:** Design for diverse abilities and accessibility
    requirements.
-   **Learner agency:** Offer choices in how learners interact with
    content.
-   **Responsible AI:** Treat AI-generated guidance as fallible and
    support verification.
-   **Privacy:** Collect only necessary information and protect learner
    data.
-   **Meaningful gamification:** Encourage understanding and progress,
    not just screen time or speed.
